# Robotics and technology education providers in BH/RMBH that take children (ages 8-11) to national and international competitions (excluding Robótica Dhel)

Research date: 18 Oct 2026. Scope: Belo Horizonte and RMBH. Robótica Dhel is profiled by another researcher. It appears below only as a benchmark, because its team Amigos Droids dominates the BH competition news.

Overall picture: Outside Dhel, I found **no independent (non-school) robotics provider in BH with documented competition teams or trips**. The franchises (Ctrl+Play, SuperGeeks/ex-CodeBuddy, Mind Makers) appear to be course-only. Among private colégios, the clearest current competition evidence is **Colégio Bernoulli**, which had many OBR teams in 2026 and one Nível 1 team qualified for the OBR national in Nov 2026, and **Colégio ICJ** (OBR nationals 2022, TBR, FIRA). The **SESI MG schools in BH** also field FLL teams that reached the SESI national (2025). The big MG FLL winners are mostly SESI schools in the interior and public schools in Betim. No BH colégio was found with a 2024-2026 international FLL/WRO/RoboCup Junior trip for children aged 8-11.

- I could not verify whether any franchise unit has ever registered an FLL "garage team" or a TBR team.

## 3. Can an external child join a colégio's competition team? Community/independent teams in BH

### Takeaway
FIRST LEGO League (Explore 6-10, Challenge 9-16) explicitly allows "equipes de garagem" (community teams of friends) with two adult coaches. This is the main route for a child from any school, and it is how Dhel's Amigos Droids competes with students from different schools. OBR registration must go through a teacher or tutor at an educational institution. I found no evidence of any BH colégio accepting non-enrolled children onto its competition teams.

### Cited Findings
- SESI (official FLL operator in Brazil since 2013, with FIRST and LEGO Education) says a team can be formed "com amigos da rua", i.e., an "equipe de garagem". Teams have 2-10 students aged 9-16, at least two adult coaches recommended, and registration by coaches or teachers over 18. — [SESI FLL Challenge](https://www.sesi.portaldaindustria.com.br/para-voce/robotica/first-lego-league-challenge); [FIEMG](https://www.fiemg.com.br/fiemg/noticias/escola-sesi-granbery-recebe-etapa-microrregional-de-um-dos-maiores-torneios-de-robotica-do-mundo/)
- **FLL Explore**: ages 6-10. Applies to curricular and extracurricular settings and to garage teams with no institutional link. Teams present a "Show Me Poster" at a celebration event. Requires the season's LEGO sets. Contact: firstlegoleague@positivo.com.br. — [FLL Brasil – Explore](https://www.firstlegoleague.com.br/first-lego-league-explore)
- FLL categories by age: Discover 3-6, Explore 6-10, Challenge 9-16. — [FIEMG](https://www.fiemg.com.br/fiemg/noticias/escola-sesi-granbery-recebe-etapa-microrregional-de-um-dos-maiores-torneios-de-robotica-do-mundo/)
- An earlier MG FLL state stage in BH (UniBH) had 37 teams "de escolas públicas, particulares e times de garagem" (c. 2018). — [Portal da Indústria (Lego Bros)](https://noticias.portaldaindustria.com.br/noticias/educacao/lego-bros-vence-a-etapa-mineira-do-torneio-de-robotica/)
- Amigos Droids placed 3rd in the Champion's Award at the 2022-23 national as a "garage team" from BH. — [Portal da Indústria 2023 winners (search summary)](https://noticias.portaldaindustria.com.br/noticias/robotica/os-grandes-vencedores-do-festival-sesi-de-robotica-2023/)
- **OBR**: registration is free and made by a teacher or tutor of an educational institution, for students from public or private schools. The theoretical modality is split by grade. — [search summary of OBR rules, Marista Natal circular](https://marista.edu.br/natal/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/019-DIR-OLIMPIADA-DE-ROBOTICA-OBR-MODALIDADE-TEORICA.pdf)
- **WRO Elementary**: ages 8-12. The Brazil national qualifies teams for the international final. — [NOIC – WRO](https://noic.com.br/olimpiadas/robotica/wro/)
- **TBR**: open and free for public schools, private schools and social projects. — [search summary](https://www.cep.pr.gov.br/Noticia/Estudantes-do-CEP-de-robotica-criam-solucoes-para-os-desafios-globais-da-ONU-unindo)

### Inferences
- Outside Dhel, the main way an 8-9-year-old from any school can reach FLL competitions is a parent- or coach-led **garage team**: FLL Explore now, FLL Challenge from 9. It could be organized with a robotics school's support, e.g., asking Ctrl+Play/SuperGeeks whether they would coach one, but no BH franchise was found doing this.
- Colégio teams (Bernoulli, ICJ, SESI, Batista) are almost certainly limited to enrolled students. Joining one would mean changing schools.

### Gaps

- 2026/27 FLL Explore event locations in MG were not listed on the FLL Brasil page.
- Exact OBR age/grade rules for the practical Nível 1 were not verified in this session.
