# Robótica DHEL (BH) e o evento LEGO no Rio de Janeiro (17/10/2026)

Research date: 18 Oct 2026. Every claim below links to its source. Older information is marked with its year. Conflicts between sources are flagged where they appear.

## 1. Who Robótica DHEL is: founders, history, unit, age tracks, methodology and kits, class format, prices

### Takeaway
DHEL ("DHEL | Formação em Tecnologia", also called "Escola de Criação, Prototipagem e Robótica") is a single-unit extracurricular robotics and technology school. It was founded in 2010 by Débora Oliveira Lana and Sérgio Lana and is located in Santo Agostinho/Lourdes (Centro-Sul, BH). It serves ages 4 to 18 in age-based tracks. An 8-year-old belongs in the **DHEL ONE** track (8 to 10 years), and can also join DHEL's new **FLL Future Edition** competition teams (8 to 14 years). Course prices are not published.

## 2. Competitions DHEL attends and its track record

### Takeaway
DHEL is a FIRST-ecosystem school: FLL Explore, FLL Challenge (historically), FTC, and its own Robocopa. It does not appear to compete in OBR, WRO, RoboCup Jr/CBR or MNR. Its flagship team **Amigos Droids (FTC #17792)**, formed at DHEL, won the **Inspire Award, the top FTC prize, at the FIRST Championship in Houston in May 2026**. O Tempo reports this was the first Brazilian team to do so. In 2026 DHEL also co-organized the **FLL Explore Minas Gerais regional** and placed two Explore teams in the national stage.

## 3. Trips: how many per year, how they are organized, costs

### Takeaway
DHEL publicly runs a **paid US competition-trip program, "Imersão Robótica EUA", for ages 14–17**. It also takes competition teams to the national FLL Explore and Future Edition stage in Rio, and the Amigos Droids team travels to the US roughly once or twice a year. For an 8-year-old, the realistic cadence is **about 1 trip per year**: the FLL national in Rio, if the team qualifies or joins the Future Edition team. An international trip at that age would require winning the national stage. No public information was found on how DHEL organizes the Rio trip for young children, such as parent escorts or costs.

### Cited Findings
- **Imersão Robótica EUA 2026** (DHEL; "fundadores Débora e Sérgio Lana"):
  - Eligibility: ages **14–17**, limited to **10–12 students** per cohort.
  - Includes a 120-hour course over 15 weeks (2 × 2h per week plus 8 Saturday immersions).
  - Turma 1: course started 26 Jan 2026, trip **June 2026, Chicago** (tournament at South Bend).
  - Turma 2: course starts 27 Jul 2026, trip **Feb 2027, Philadelphia**.
  - The trip lasts 10 days, including an **FTC** tournament plus university and cultural visits.
  - Price "a partir de **US$ 3.990**", up to 6× interest-free. Included: course, a loaned FTC Starter kit, event fees, quad-room lodging, rental-car transport and snacks. **Airfare, visa/passport, insurance, meals and attractions are not included.**
  - The FAQ asks "Os pais podem acompanhar?" but no answer is visible.

### Inferences
- The yearly trip ladder for a DHEL child today looks like this:
  - **Ages 6–10:** FLL Explore regional in BH, then, if invited, the national in Rio (October). Only the national winner, and possibly 1–2 more depending on slots, goes to FIRST Championship Houston.
  - **Ages 8–14:** the FLL Future Edition national/showcase in Rio, where the top team goes to an international event in Greece in 2027.
  - **Ages 14–17:** Imersão EUA (two cohorts per year), plus FTC with Amigos Droids (national Festival SESI and Houston).
- So "1–2 trips per year" is realistic at DHEL only from about age 14. Before that, expect about one domestic trip per year (Rio), with a possible international trip if the team wins.
- The Imersão EUA cost (US$3,990 plus airfare and meals) suggests a total of roughly US$6–7k per trip. **This is an estimate, not a reported figure.**

- No information on scholarships for the Imersão.

## 4. What the Rio de Janeiro "encontro da LEGO" was (17 Oct 2026)

### Takeaway
The event was almost certainly the **FIRST® LEGO® League national stage for Brazil, 2026 season**. It combined the national **FLL Explore** celebration (ages 6–10, theme UNEARTHED™) with the **Brazilian debut of FLL Future Edition**. It was held **Saturday 17 Oct 2026, 9h–18h, at the Ginásio Educacional Olímpico Isabel Salgado, Barra Olímpica, Rio de Janeiro**, and operated by **Educacional (Positivo Tecnologia)** with LEGO Education. SESI was not the organizer. DHEL had two Explore teams invited (DHELego and LEGO® Engineers) and advertised Future Edition teams for this same national stage. DHEL's participation is therefore highly likely, though no source names DHEL at the event.

## 5. FLL in Brazil 2026–2027: context for an 8-year-old (brief; another researcher covers FLL in depth)

### Takeaway
Brazil runs FLL in two tracks.
- **FLL Explore** (ages 6–10, SPIKE Essential) and the new **Future Edition** are operated by **Educacional/Positivo** on a calendar-year cycle ending in an October national in Rio.
- **FLL Challenge** (ages 9–16 per the FAQ, SPIKE Prime), FTC and FRC are run by **SESI** on the FIRST season cycle. The 2026/27 season is "FIRST CANOPY", with the BIOGLOW theme for FLL. Regionals run through March 2027, and the national **Festival SESI de Robótica** is in **Salvador, 18–21 March 2027**, feeding Houston in April.

An 8-year-old fits **Explore** or **Future Edition (8–10 band)**, but is generally too young for Challenge.

### Cited Findings
- Educacional says the **BIOGLOW™** season, part of FIRST® CANOPY™, was launched 4 Aug 2026 and is the "última temporada da FIRST LEGO League em sua estrutura atual". — [Educacional – FLL 2026–2027](https://educacional.com.br/noticia/first-lego-league-2026-2027/)
- Educacional's categories:
  - Founders Edition Explore: about 6–10 years, **SPIKE Essential**
  - Founders Edition Challenge: Grades 4–8 in the table but **9–16 years** in the FAQ (internal inconsistency), **SPIKE Prime**
  - Future Edition: bands of 5–7, **8–10** and 11–14 years, **LEGO Education Computer Science & AI** kit
  - Source: [Educacional – FLL 2026–2027](https://educacional.com.br/noticia/first-lego-league-2026-2027/)
- Educacional's calendar:
  - Explore national celebration and Future Edition showcase: 17/10/2026, Rio
  - Explore 2027 registrations: Feb 2027
  - Challenge regionals: Oct 2026–Jan 2027
  - Challenge national: Mar 2027
  - Source: [Educacional – FLL 2026–2027](https://educacional.com.br/noticia/first-lego-league-2026-2027/)
- SESI (6 Oct 2026) says the FIRST CANOPY 2026/27 season has 30+ regional qualifiers in 18 states through March 2027, with the first in Recife. The **9th Festival SESI de Robótica** (FLLC, FTC, FRC, STEM Racing) runs **18–21 Mar 2027 in Salvador**, and its best teams go to the FIRST Championship Houston in April. SESI says it has run FLL Challenge for 15 years. — [Agência de Notícias da Indústria, 06/10/2026](https://noticias.portaldaindustria.com.br/noticias/educacao/confira-o-calendario-de-competicoes-regionais-dos-torneios-sesi-de-robotica/)
- Last season's SESI MG regional (FTC event "BRBHQ") was held in Belo Horizonte, 30 Oct–1 Nov 2025. — [FTC Events BRBHQ 2025](https://ftc-events.firstinspires.org/2025/BRBHQ)
- High Tech Kids (US) says Founders Edition continues through 2026–27 and 2027–28, and Future Edition becomes the standard in 2028–29. — [High Tech Kids – FLL Future Edition](https://hightechkids.org/fll-future-edition/)
  - This conflicts with Educacional's statement that BIOGLOW is the "last season in the current structure". The wording may differ by region.

### Inferences
- Brazil's Explore national uses the *previous* FIRST theme: the Oct 2026 Rio national used UNEARTHED (2025/26). The 2027 Explore cycle will likely use BIOGLOW.
- For an 8-year-old at DHEL, the next steps are:
  1. Explore 2027 (registration Feb 2027, MG regional around May–Jun, national around Oct 2027), **or**
  2. Future Edition (8–10 band).
- He reaches FLL Challenge, and with it the SESI regional/national ladder, at about 9–10.

### Gaps
- Brazil-specific FLL Challenge minimum age for 2026/27 was not confirmed from SESI. Educacional's page is internally inconsistent.
- Future Edition's Brazilian competition structure after 2026 (showcase vs. competitive national) is unclear.
