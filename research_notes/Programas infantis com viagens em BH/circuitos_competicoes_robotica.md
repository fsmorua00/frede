## 1. FIRST LEGO League in Brazil: Explore (ages 6-10) and Challenge (ages 9-15), operators, ladders, 2026-27 calendar, international slots

### Takeaway
Brazil splits FIRST LEGO League between two operators. **Educacional** (a Positivo Tecnologia unit) runs **Discover (3-6) and Explore (6-10)**. **SESI** runs **Challenge (9-15)**. For an 8-year-old, **FLL Explore is the only FIRST path open now**, and in Brazil it is selective: regional festivals lead to a national stage, and the national winners get a slot at the **FIRST Championship in Houston (28 Apr – 1 May 2027)**. The **national Explore stage was held in Rio de Janeiro on 17 Oct 2026**, which is almost certainly the event the child just attended. From age 9 the child can join FLL Challenge. Its ladder for the 2026-27 season is: MG state tournament in Belo Horizonte (27-29 Nov 2026) → 9th Festival SESI de Robótica in Salvador (18-21 Mar 2027) → Houston (Apr 2027) or FLL Open Invitationals abroad.

### Cited Findings
**Operators and age bands**
- In Brazil, FLL Discover (ages 3-6) and Explore (ages 6-10) are operated by Educacional (Positivo Tecnologia S.A.), and FLL Challenge is operated by SESI Departamento Nacional. Discover and Explore were introduced in Brazil in 2018. Challenge has operated in Brazil since 2004 (the page gives its age band as 9-15). — [firstlegoleague.com.br](https://www.firstlegoleague.com.br/)
- SESI's official FLL Challenge page gives the following rules:
  - ages **9 to 15**;
  - teams of **2 to 10 competitors** (recommended minimum 4), each led by **2 adult coaches**;
  - a competitor may be on only one FLL Challenge team per season;
  - regional tournaments run **October to February** in several states plus the DF, with 34 tournaments in 19 states + DF last season;

  - registration links "em breve" (no fee published). — [SESI – FLL Challenge](https://www.sesi.portaldaindustria.com.br/para-voce/robotica/first-lego-league-challenge)

- **Ladder per year for an MG Explore team:** MG regional festival in BH (around May) → national (around October, recently Rio) → Houston (following April/May). That is potentially one national and one international trip per season, but only for national winners.
- **At age 9, FLL Challenge opens** (SESI 9-15). MG teams currently compete in BH for the state tournament, so the first FLLC trip would be the national festival, which rotates: Brasília 2023, São Paulo 2026, Salvador 2027.

## 2. WRO (World Robot Olympiad) Brazil: categories, age rules, national final, international final, Brazilian results

### Takeaway
WRO is the second circuit open to an 8-year-old right now. Its **RoboMission Elementary** age group covers ages 8-12 (born 2014-2018 for the 2026 season), with teams of only 2-3 students plus a coach. WRO Brasil is run by **Robomind**. Registration for 2026 is closed, and the **next registrations open in 2027**. In 2026 Brazil sent an **Elementary team (Astrogenius Orion) to the WRO International Final in San Juan, Puerto Rico, 8-10 Dec 2026**, and Elementary teams also went to the **Open Championship Americas, California, 25-27 Sep 2026**.

### Cited Findings
- **WRO 2026 RoboMission general rules:**
  - age groups **Elementary 8-12 (born 2014-2018 in season 2026)**, **Junior 11-15 (born 2011-2015)**, **Senior 14-19 (born 2007-2012)**; maximum age is the age turned in the calendar year;
  - a team has **2 or 3 students** and a coach; one student plus one coach is not a team;
  - each student may be on only one team, and a team may enter only one WRO category per season;
  - the coach must be 18+ at international events;
  - Elementary robots are limited to 4 motors.
  - Source: [WRO 2026 RoboMission General Rules (PDF, 15 Jan 2026)](https://wro-association.org/wp-content/uploads/WRO-2026-RoboMission-General-Rules.pdf)
- The 2026 Elementary game rules note that national organizers may simplify missions and that a surprise mission for the International Final was released on 8 Oct 2026. — [WRO 2026 RoboMission Elementary Game Rules](https://wro-association.org/wp-content/uploads/WRO-2026-RoboMission-Elementary-Game-Rules.pdf)
- **WRO Brasil (operated by Robomind):**
  - ages 8-19; categories RoboMission (Elementary, Junior, Senior), Future Innovators and RoboSports; 2026 theme "Robôs Encontram a Cultura";
  - **"2026 registration closed; next registrations open only in 2027"**;
  - Open Championship Americas, **25-27 Sep 2026, California**: Brazilian teams All Might Kids (Future Innovators Elementary), Anonymous Alpha and Anonymous Sigma (RoboMission Senior), Astrogenius (RoboMission Elementary);
  - **World Final 8-10 Dec 2026, Puerto Rico**: Astrogenius Orion (RoboMission Elementary), Cyberdragons (Junior), Anonymous Sigma (Senior), CCR Masters (RoboSports), All Might Kids (Future Innovators).
  - Source: [WRO Brasil / Robomind](https://lp.robomind.com.br/wro-brasil/)
- The WRO Association confirms the 2026 International Final will be in **San Juan, Puerto Rico, 8-10 Dec 2026**. Teams can qualify **only through their own country's national WRO competition**, and the number of slots per country depends on the number of teams in that country. — [WRO Association – International Events](https://wro-association.org/competition/international-events/)
- **Earlier seasons:**
  - **2025:** the Brazilian national stage was held in **Jaraguá do Sul (SC)**, organized by Robomind, and qualified students for international events in **Singapore** (2025 World Final) and **Panama**. — [Diário do Rio](https://diariodorio.com/estudantes-do-rj-se-classificam-para-olimpiada-de-robotica-no-panama-e-singapaura/)
  - **2024:** team registration was via wrobr.com.br/equipes (search-result summary of the same coverage).

### Inferences
- **Eligibility:** for 2027, Elementary will presumably cover birth years 2015-2019 (the rules shift one year each season), so an 8-year-old born 2018 would be eligible for WRO Elementary through about the 2030 season (age 12).
- **Team size:** with only 2-3 kids per team, WRO is easier to form around one family or a small academy group than FLL Challenge (up to 10).
- **Typical annual rhythm:** national selection mid-year (exact 2027 date unknown) → Open Championship Americas in Sep (USA) and/or World Final in Nov/Dec (host rotates: Singapore 2025, Puerto Rico 2026). That is potentially one national and one international trip per year.
- **MG presence:** no MG WRO team was found in the 2026 Brazilian international list. Team origins were not given.

## 3. OBR (Olimpíada Brasileira de Robótica), CBR, MNR and the RoboCup Junior path

### Takeaway
OBR is **free** and **open to a 3rd/4th-grader now**:
- **Practical (Rescue) modality:** Nível 0 covers the 1st-3rd year (regional/state only, playful format). Nível 1 covers the 1st-8th year and **can reach the national stage**.
- **Theory modality:** individual exam in school. Nível 1 covers the 2nd-3rd year and Nível 2 the 4th-5th year.

The 2026 cycle in MG was:
- **BH regional** at CEFET-MG on 4 Jul 2026;
- **MG state stage** in São João del-Rei on 29 Aug 2026;
- **national stage** in **João Pessoa (PB), 23-29 Nov 2026**, alongside CBR and MNR ("Robótica 2026", free entry).

The **international RoboCup Junior path is only for Nível 2** (8th grade+, about 13+). RoboCup 2027 will be in **Nuremberg, Germany, 15-21 Jun 2027**.

### Cited Findings
- **OBR 2026 Practical modalities manual:**
  - **Nível 0:** students enrolled in **1st, 2nd or 3rd year** of Ensino Fundamental; only in **Robótica de Resgate** (in person); takes part **only in regional/state stages**.
  - **Nível 1:** 1st to 8th year; regional/state stages and **can qualify for the National stage**.
  - **Nível 2:** 8th-9th year, high school or technical-integrated; can qualify for the National stage **and compete for a place at the RoboCup Jr international stage**.
  - The level is determined by **school year, not age**; maximum age is 19.
  - Students are registered by a **teacher/tutor ("técnico")** through Sistema Olimpo. Coaches not linked to a school may enter **garage teams**.
  - A student may be on one in-person team (Resgate or Artística) plus one Virtual team. Team members cannot change after qualifying.
  - **2026 dates:** registrations 11 Mar – 30 Apr; regional/state in-person stages 29 May – 13 Sep; **National 23-27 Nov**.
  - "**OBR is a public and free initiative**" (no fee).
  - Source: [OBR 2026 MP Manual de Inscrição (PDF)](https://obr.robocup.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/OBR2026_MP_Manual-de-Inscricao.pdf)
  - The OBR site says Nível 0 has differentiated rules and awards in a "less competitive, more playful" environment (per search summary of [OBR Modalidades Práticas](https://obr.robocup.org.br/modalidade-pratica/)).
- **OBR theoretical modality:**
  - individual exam applied in the schools on the same day nationwide;
  - **Nível 0 = 1st year; Nível 1 = 2nd-3rd year; Nível 2 = 4th-5th year; Nível 3 = 6th-7th; Nível 4 = 8th-9th; Nível 5 = high school**;
  - Levels 1-5 have two phases;
  - awards are national gold/silver/bronze medals, Mérito Estadual, places in a Minicurso de Robótica, and CNPq Iniciação Científica Jr. scholarships. No trip-based national final is described.
  - Source: [OBR – Modalidade Teórica](https://obr.robocup.org.br/modalidade-teorica/)

### Inferences
- **For an 8-year-old in the 3rd year (2026) or 4th year (2027):** OBR Prática **Nível 1 Resgate** is the highest-upside option. It is free, team-based via a tutor (school or garage team), and the BH regional is local at CEFET-MG (early July). The state stage requires a domestic trip (São João del-Rei in 2026), and a national stage in late November (João Pessoa in 2026) gives a long-distance domestic trip. Nível 0 would cap the child at the regional/state stage.
- An MG Nível 1 team (Orion_Bernoulli, a Colégio Bernoulli team, plus R2-D2 and Millenium Bots) reached the 2026 national stage, so MG elementary-level teams do advance.
- **International path via OBR (RoboCup Junior world event) opens only at Nível 2 (8th grade, about 13 years old).** For the child this would be around 2031-32.
- The MNR is a project showcase held with the OBR/CBR national event. It could give an extra domestic trip if a school project is accepted.

## 6. Team-based vs individual; age ladder for a child who is 8 now; minimum age to reach the international stage

### Takeaway
Every circuit with travel is **team-based with an adult coach**. The child must join a school, academy or "garage" team. **OBR Theory** is the only individual option, and it has no travel final. The earliest realistic international paths are:
- **FLL Explore → Houston** (ages 6-10; available now);
- **WRO RoboMission Elementary → Open Americas / World Final** (ages 8-12; available from the 2027 season);
- **FLL Challenge → Houston / Opens** (from age 9).

### Cited Findings
- **FLL Challenge:** teams of 2-10 with 2 adult coaches; may represent a school, club, organization or group of friends. — [SESI](https://www.sesi.portaldaindustria.com.br/para-voce/robotica/first-lego-league-challenge); [Agência de Notícias da Indústria (older)](https://noticias.portaldaindustria.com.br/noticias/robotica/contagem-regressiva-ultimas-semanas-de-inscricoes-para-as-competicoes-da-fll/)
- **FLL Explore:** up to 6 members, ages 6-10. — [Jornal do Brás](https://jornaldobras.com.br/noticia/82783/tecnologia-ciencia-e-arqueologia-se-encontram-na-nova-temporada-do-maior-torneio-de-robotica-educacional)
- **WRO:** 2-3 students plus a coach. — [WRO General Rules 2026](https://wro-association.org/wp-content/uploads/WRO-2026-RoboMission-General-Rules.pdf)
- **OBR Prática:** registered by a técnico (teacher/tutor); garage teams allowed. **OBR Teórica** is per-student, in school. — [OBR manual](https://obr.robocup.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/OBR2026_MP_Manual-de-Inscricao.pdf); [OBR Teórica](https://obr.robocup.org.br/modalidade-teorica/)

## 8. Costs: entry fees and typical travel costs

### Takeaway
Entry fees in Brazil are low or zero: OBR is free, MNR/Robótica 2026 has free entry, and SESI FLL was historically free. The real cost is **academy/team membership, kits, and travel**. International trips (Houston) have historically cost on the order of **US$2,000+ per person** in older reports. Current 2026-27 fees were not found.

### Cited Findings
- OBR: "public and free initiative; no payment needed". — [OBR manual 2026](https://obr.robocup.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/OBR2026_MP_Manual-de-Inscricao.pdf)
- Robótica 2026 (OBR/CBR/MNR national, João Pessoa): free entry. — [Vestibulando Web](https://www.vestibulandoweb.com.br/ensino/robotica-2026/amp/)
- SESI FLL: free participation in the 2020-21 edition, and free spectator entry at the MG regional in 2025. — [Agência de Notícias da Indústria (older)](https://noticias.portaldaindustria.com.br/noticias/robotica/inscricoes-abertas-para-o-maior-torneio-de-robotica-do-brasil--/); [FIEMG 2025](https://www.fiemg.com.br/sesi/noticias/etapa-regional-minas-gerais-reuniu-mais-de-800-participantes-e-garantiu-vaga-para-o-torneio-nacional-em-sao-paulo/)
- **Travel cost examples (older seasons, about 2019-2023; exact years not verified):**
  - Taubaté (SP) FRC team: about **US$2,000 each** for at least 5 students + 1 mentor to go to the world championship. — [Agência de Notícias da Indústria](https://noticias.portaldaindustria.com.br/noticias/educacao/equipe-de-robotica-de-taubate-sp-ganha-vaga-mas-pode-nao-ir-a-mundial-nos-estados-unidos/)
  - Foz do Iguaçu team "Strike" (20+ members): **US$6,000 registration + R$30,000 travel**. — [Agência de Notícias da Indústria](https://noticias.portaldaindustria.com.br/noticias/robotica/vakinha-ta-on-equipe-de-robotica-arrecada-dinheiro-para-competir-em-mundial/)
  - Porto Alegre public school: the city invested **R$140,000** for 6 students + teacher. — [Prefeitura de Porto Alegre](https://prefeitura.poa.br/smed/noticias/prefeitura-garante-recursos-para-viagem-de-alunos-torneio-mundial-de-robotica-nos-eua)

### Inferences
- **Estimated budget for a parent travelling with an 8-10-year-old to Houston** (5-6 nights, flights from BH via a hub, hotel, meals, and a share of team registration): likely about R$20,000-35,000 for child plus one parent at 2026-27 prices. This is my estimate, based on the ~US$2k/person and ~R$20k/person precedents above plus a parent ticket. It is not a sourced figure.
- **Domestic national finals** (Salvador for the Festival SESI in March 2027; João Pessoa for OBR in November 2026; Rio for Explore in October 2026): likely about R$3,000-7,000 per child + parent for flights and 3-4 hotel nights. This is also an unsourced estimate.
