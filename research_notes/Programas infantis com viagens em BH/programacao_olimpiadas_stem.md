# Non-robotics tech/STEM programs, olympiads, fairs and camps with national/international trips for an 8-year-old in Belo Horizonte (as of 18 Oct 2026)

Child profile used for age gating: 8 years old, probably in 3º ano EF in 2026, so 4º ano in 2027 and 5º ano in 2028. Robotics circuits (FLL/WRO/OBR/RoboCup/VEX) are out of scope. Chess is covered elsewhere.

## 1. Programming / computational-thinking competitions for kids, national and international (OBI, Bebras, Scratch, Minecraft, Coding Olympiads, Codeavour, Technovation, hackathons)

### Takeaway
None of the Brazilian national programming contests open to an 8–10-year-old (OBI Iniciação Júnior, Bebras) involves travel at this age. They are free or cheap, run on paper or online, and are entered through the school. The travel opportunities in coding are international private olympiads that families can enter as individuals. The strongest is the **Coding Olympiad (Main Team Organization / Competzy)**: grades 1–12 (grades 3–11 for the Rome final), online qualifiers from **31 Oct 2026**, and in-person **Global Rounds in Orlando–Miami (1–6 Feb 2027) and Lisbon/New York (Jun–Jul 2027)**. **Codeavour** (ages 7–18) is a secondary option with no Brazilian partner found.

### Cited Findings
**OBI – Olimpíada Brasileira de Informática (Unicamp/SBC)**
- OBI2026 modalities: **Iniciação** (paper, multiple-choice logic and computational reasoning). **Nível Júnior = 4º and 5º ano EF**, Nível 1 = 6º–7º, Nível 2 = 8º–9º. **Programação** (on computer, in Python/C/C++/Java). The **new "Mirim" level (OBI2026) is for students "up to the 7º ano"**, Júnior is 8º–9º, Nível 1 ends at 1º ano EM, and Nível 2 ends at 3º ano EM. The site encourages Iniciação students to move to Programação as soon as possible. — [OBI official site](https://olimpiada.ic.unicamp.br/)
- OBI participation is **free** for schools and competitors. A teacher registers the school, receives a password, and then registers the students. Fase 1 preliminary results came out 22 Jun 2026. Final results and the medal table are listed for **22 Oct 2026**, and an "OBI2026 event" is listed for **12 Dec 2026**. The site mentions a Fase Local and a Fase Nacional with venues listed per modality. It mentions a "Semana Olímpica da OBI" only as an invitation prize for top placers in the Competição Feminina. — [OBI official site](https://olimpiada.ic.unicamp.br/)
- 2025 Iniciação Júnior calendar for reference: Fase 1 on 5–7 Jun 2025 and Fase 2 on 7–8 Aug 2025, each one hour (per search summary of published exam booklets). — [OBI 2025 Júnior exam booklet](https://olimpiada.ic.unicamp.br/static/extras/obi2025/provas/ProvaOBI2025_f1ij.pdf)
- Historically the top placers attend a final/"Olympic week" at Unicamp, and a Unicamp page describes participants as **11 to 18 years old** from across Brazil (per search excerpt). — [IC Unicamp page](https://ic.unicamp.br/en/?p=4821)

**Desafio Bebras Brasil (computational thinking)**
- Organized in Brazil by **UpMat Educacional**. Multiple-choice tests are for students **from 3º ano EF**, public or private, and need no prior computing knowledge. In 2023 schools could register **until 1 Nov** and the challenge ran in November (2023 info). — [Blog do Vestibular, 2023](https://www.blogdovestibular.com/noticias/desafio-bebras-inscricoes-abertas-para-testar-pensamento-computacional-em-estudantes.html); [Catraca Livre](https://catracalivre.com.br/educacao/desafio-computacional/)

**Coding Olympiad (Main Team Organization – "International STEM & Coding Olympiad"; sold in some countries via Competzy/Eduversal)**
- Organizer: **Main Team Organization (MTO)**, founded 2012, based in Germany/USA. Open to **grades 3 to 11**. Students qualify through several online rounds. The **2026 Grand Final was 2–8 July 2026 at Università Campus Bio-Medico di Roma**, with **700+ students in person from 54 countries**. The final moves to a different city each year. Kodland is a coding-course company, so the article is promotional. — [Kodland blog](https://www.kodland.org/blog/stem-coding-olympiad-grand-final-rome-2026); [Università Campus Bio-Medico news](https://www.unicampus.it/en/news/luniversita-campus-bio-medico-di-roma-al-centro-della-formazione-internazionale-oltre-700-studenti-da-54-paesi-per-la-grand-final-delle-stem-olympiad/)
- A **nine-year-old** Thai boy won two world coding titles at the Rome final (July 2026), which shows that children of about 9 compete in person at the final. — [Khaosod English, 16 Jul 2026](https://www.khaosodenglish.com/featured/2026/07/16/nine-year-old-thai-boy-wins-two-world-coding-titles-in-rome/)
- The Competzy portal for the "Coding Olympiad 2026–2027" covers **grades 1–12**. Tracks: **Challenges by Codementum** (grades 1–12; block coding, Python, JavaScript), **Arena Battles by CodeCombat** (grades 4–12; Python/JS) and **AI Prompt Lab** (grades 6–11). **Schedule: Online Round 1 31 Oct–1 Nov 2026; Online Round 2 19–20 Dec 2026; Global Round Orlando–Miami (USA) 1–6 Feb 2027; Online Round 3 20–21 Feb 2027; Global Round Lisbon (Portugal) / New York (USA) Jun–Jul 2027.** Registration is online and open now. Fees are not shown. The portal claims 72+ countries and does not mention Brazil. — [Competzy Coding Olympiad](https://codingolympiad.competzy.com/)
- The codingolympiad.org site states "One Registration, Five Olympiads" and claims 72+ countries, 405 schools and 9,700 students. It lists Arena Battles, Challenges, AI Prompt Engineering and a free Computer Science track, and links fee policy and exam-schedule PDFs (amounts not readable). The 2025–26 Codementum schedule had qualification options on 1 Nov 2025, 24 Jan 2026 and 7 Mar 2026, plus an online final on 29 Mar 2026. — [codingolympiad.org](https://codingolympiad.org/)

### Inferences
- The OBI Programação **Mirim** level is defined only as "up to the 7º ano", with no lower bound on the page. An 8–9-year-old who already codes in Python could probably be registered by his school, which would give a stronger signal than Iniciação Júnior. Confirm with OBI. Iniciação Júnior applies from 4º ano (2027).
- No OBI travel should be expected before the Programação levels and ages 11+. The "Semana Olímpica" at Unicamp is the long-term goal.
- The Coding Olympiad is the realistic route to an **international coding trip in 2027**. Steps: register now and take Online Round 1 on 31 Oct–1 Nov 2026 (Codementum block coding suits age 8). If he qualifies, travel to Orlando–Miami (1–6 Feb 2027) or Lisbon/New York (Jun–Jul 2027). Competzy's track list matches codingolympiad.org's, which suggests it is a regional reseller of the same MTO competition. Families can enter individually, with no school needed (inferred from the online registration).
- Codeavour would also accept him (age 7+), but without a Brazilian national partner the entry route from BH is unclear.

### Gaps
- Coding Olympiad fees (registration and Global Round participation) and whether Brazil has a national partner. The fee PDFs were not readable.
- Bebras Brasil 2026 dates and fees, which may already be closed for 2026. No 2026 Brazilian source found.
- Whether OBI's 12 Dec 2026 event is an in-person ceremony and who is invited. Whether Iniciação Júnior medalists ever travel.
- No Brazilian Roblox/game-jam competition for kids with a national final found. No Code.org event with travel found. No "World Coding Olympiad" or "International Olympiad of Junior Coders" with a verifiable Brazilian route found.

## 2. Coding/game schools in BH with internal festivals, championships or trips

### Takeaway
No evidence was found of SuperGeeks, Happy Code, Ctrl+Play, Code Kids, Ensina Code, Programaê or Ilha da Programação BH units running championships that include **trips**. The only recurring items found are one-off events: a Happy Code × AuthenticGames workshop in BH, a game jam by GameXperts (location unconfirmed), and the state's **PlayMinas** games circuit, which ends at Expominas BH.

- **PlayMinas** (Codemge / Governo de MG) is an itinerant project that uses games as a learning tool. The 2026 circuit started in **Venda Nova (BH)**, and its closing festival was set for **Expominas, 18–21 Jun 2026**, with an amateur e-sports championship. — [Diário do Comércio](https://diariodocomercio.com.br/variedades/playminas-circuito-2026-venda-nova/)

## 3. Math and science olympiads for ages 8–11 with travel (OBMEP Mirim, Canguru, SASMO/SIMOC, WMI, WMTC, ITMC/AIMO via Matemática sem Fronteiras, OBA/MOBFOG, mental math)

### Takeaway
Brazilian olympiads for this age (OBMEP Mirim, Canguru, OBA/MOBFOG) **do not include travel**. The rocket "Jornada de Foguetes" travel opens only at **6º ano**. Travel at ages 9–11 comes through **international math olympiads entered as school teams**. **BH municipal schools have already sent 4º–5º graders**: WMTC national stage in Campinas, then a global final in Doha (2024) or Bangkok (Dec 2025). The **Matemática sem Fronteiras (OIMSF)** class-team olympiad, base level from **4º ano**, feeds invitations to **ITMC (Bangkok), AIMO (Hanoi) and the MSF Grande Finale (France)**. SASMO→SIMOC (Singapore, July) and WMI (Japan, July 2026) are further routes, but no Brazilian organizer was identified. Trips are usually paid by families or, for public schools, by the city or state.

### Cited Findings
**OBMEP Mirim (IMPA) – no travel**
- **Mirim 1 = 2º–3º ano; Mirim 2 = 4º–5º ano.** Schools register (MEC/INEP code) by **8 Jun 2026**. 1st phase 25 Aug 2026; **2nd phase 10 Nov 2026**. 15 multiple-choice questions. Awards are digital certificates, or medals if the school pays for the medal option. No travel or ceremony is mentioned. — [Quero Bolsa](https://querobolsa.com.br/revista/obmep-mirim-2026); [Official regulation](https://olimpiadamirim.obmep.org.br/static/media/regulamento.8e507e85.pdf)

**Canguru de Matemática Brasil (Kangaroo) – no travel**
- **Nível P (Pré-Écolier) = 3º and 4º ano**; Nível E = 5º–6º. P and E have 24 questions. Only schools register (MEC/INEP). For 2026 registration ran **1 Dec 2025–13 Mar 2026**, and the standard package cost **R$169 (public) / R$419 (private)**. — [Estratégia Vestibulares – edital 2026](https://vestibulares.estrategia.com/portal/noticias/canguru-matematica-brasil-2026-edital/); [Estratégia – edital 2026](https://portal.estrategia.com/militares/projeto-olimpico/edital-do-canguru-de-matematica-brasil-2026-divulgado/)
- More than 171k Brazilian students received medals in 2025. No travel prize was found. — [CNN Brasil](https://www.cnnbrasil.com.br/educacao/canguru-de-matematica-premiou-mais-de-171-mil-anos-brasileiros-em-2025/)

**WMTC – World Mathematics Team Championship (international final with BH precedent)**
- In **Aug 2025 (15–17 Aug, Campinas‑SP)**, nine students from two **BH municipal schools** took part in the WMTC Brazil stage: **EM Prof. Cláudio Brandão** (Noroeste; **two 4º ano and two 5º ano** students) and **EM Cora Coralina** (Venda Nova; 6º–9º). They won 2 silver, 2 bronze and 1 honorable mention. Medalists qualified for the **global stage in Bangkok (December 2025)**. Formats were individual, pair relay and team. Public and private schools competed, with guests from Peru. — [PBH, 19 Aug 2025](https://prefeitura.pbh.gov.br/noticias/alunos-da-rede-municipal-sao-premiados-em-olimpiada-internacional-de-matematica-0)
- 2024: EM Cora Coralina took 8 students (5º–9º ano) to the **15th WMTC in Doha, Qatar (27 Nov–2 Dec 2024)**, including **Isaac, 11, in 5º ano**. They won a silver medal (Emanuelle, 15) and an honorable mention. About 800 students from 20 countries took part. Students qualified through an internal school selection and then 6 medals at the Campinas national stage (Aug 2024). The **Prefeitura de BH supported** the trip. **OIMSF – Olimpíada Internacional Matemática sem Fronteiras (Rede POC) → ITMC / AIMO / MSF Grande Finale**
- This is the Brazilian section of *Mathématiques sans Frontières* (Strasbourg), **organized in Brazil by Rede POC International Education**. It is a **class/team competition (minimum 5 students; individual entry not allowed)**, entered only through the school with a teacher‑coordinator. Levels: **Básico = 4º–7º ano**, Júnior = 8º–9º, Sênior = EM. 2026: registration until **18 May 2026**, a single in-school test between **19–26 May 2026**, results on **10 Aug 2026**. Free for public schools; **R$1,089 per private school** (one fee regardless of number of classes). **Schools with awarded classes in 2026 are invited to join the Brazilian delegation to international olympiads in 2027.** — [Regulamento OIMSF 2026](https://www.matematicasemfronteiras.org/Regulamento_MSF_2026.pdf)
- **AIMO 2026 (Asia International Mathematical Olympiad, Hanoi, Vietnam):** São Paulo's EMEF Hélio Franco Chaves (students in 4º–9º ano) was selected to represent Brazil after a bronze at OIMSF 2025 (Southeast regional). — [Prefeitura de SP](https://prefeitura.sp.gov.br/w/de-sao-paulo-ao-cen%C3%A1rio-internacional-escola-municipal-avan%C3%A7a-para-olimp%C3%ADada-de-matem%C3%A1tica-na-%C3%A1sia)
- Rede POC (Programa de Olimpíadas Internacionais de Conhecimento) is described as Brazil's official representative for the MSF pre-selection, and in 2018 as the body that invited Brazilian students to AIMO in Bangkok (per search excerpts). — [Diário do Pará](https://diariodopara.com.br/para/alunos-de-barcarena-colocam-o-para-no-mapa-da-matematica-mundial/); [O Imparcial, 2018](https://oimparcial.com.br/cidades/2018/08/estudantes-maranhenses-sao-medalhistas-na-olimpiada-de-matematica/)
- **ITMC 2026 (International Talent Mathematics Contest), Bangkok, Feb–Mar 2026:** 12 students from EE Presidente Bernardes (Pouso Alegre‑MG) went. **SEE/MG funded the state delegation (R$1.8 million** for logistics, teaching and operations). — [Agência Minas](https://www.agenciaminas.mg.gov.br/noticia/estudantes-de-escola-estadual-de-minas-gerais-representarao-o-brasil-em-olimpiada-internacional-de-matematica-na-tailandia-128810)

### Inferences
- **Fastest math-travel path from BH for a private-school child:** have the school (or class) enter **OIMSF 2027** (registration ~May 2027, Básico level from 4º ano). An awarded class would be invited to **2028** international olympiads (ITMC/AIMO/Grande Finale). OIMSF is class-based, so the school must buy in.
- **WMTC** is a closer fit for 4º–5º graders: individual, pair and team tests, a national stage in Campinas in August, and a global final in Nov/Dec. BH municipal schools have used it twice (2024, 2025). The parent should ask whether his private school can enter, and who runs the Brazil stage (unknown).
- **SASMO→SIMOC** and **WMI** suit individual high-achievers aged 9–11, but families typically pay R$30–45k per child-plus-companion trip to Asia (inferred from the R$42k Marília case).
- The family will almost certainly pay for these trips. Public funding (PBH, SEE/MG, MEC) has so far covered only public-school delegations.
- **OBA/MOBFOG:** worth doing in 2027 at Nível 2 (4º ano) for the experience. A **rocket trip (Jornada de Foguetes, Barra do Piraí‑RJ)** becomes possible from **6º ano (2029, age ~11)**, which matches the "opens at 11" window.

### Gaps
- Brazilian organizers and registration channels for **WMTC** (Campinas stage), **WMI** and **SASMO** were not identified, so dates and fees for 2027 are unknown.
- WMI's lowest grade for Brazil is not confirmed (internationally WMI often starts at grade 3, but this was not verified).
- Exact ITMC/AIMO/WMTC 2027 dates and costs per child are unknown.
- No UCMAS/Supera Brazil data on international participation found.
- IMC Singapore and ICAS were not researched in depth. No Brazilian route was found.

## 4. Science fairs open to elementary kids with national/international credentials (Mostratec Júnior, FEBRACE, FEBIC, Ciência Jovem, MG fairs)

### Takeaway
**Mostratec Júnior** (Novo Hamburgo‑RS, late October, international) is the main fair travel option for a 9–11-year-old. **Categoria 2 = 4º–6º ano** (from 2027 for this child), and Cat 1 is presumably 1º–3º. Entry is via an affiliated fair's credential or a selection process, with a teacher as advisor. **Two MG fairs near BH accept Ensino Fundamental I and pass credentials onward:** **FECITEC – UFV Florestal** (credentials to UFMG Jovem, Ciência Jovem‑PE and LIYSF London) and **UFMG Jovem** (BH, Pampulha). **FEBRACE only opens at 8º ano.**

### Cited Findings
- **Mostratec‑Liberato 2026 (41st):** **26–30 Oct 2026, FENAC, Novo Hamburgo‑RS**. Fundamental credentialing is on 26 Oct and the **Fundamental awards ceremony is on the night of 29 Oct**. Slots: Educação Infantil 68; **Categoria 1: 34; Categoria 2: 85**; Categoria 3: 175; Categoria 4: 12. — [Regulamento Mostratec 2026 (search excerpt)](https://mostratec.liberato.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/REGULAMENTO_Mostratec_2026.pdf)
- Categories in the 2024–2026 regulations: **Categoria 2 = 4º–6º ano EF**, Categoria 3 = 7º–9º, Categoria 4 = EJA. Students must be enrolled at the matching level, and minors must be accompanied by a guardian. Categoria 1's range was not retrieved. — [Regulamento Mostratec 2026 (search excerpt)](https://mostratec.liberato.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/REGULAMENTO_Mostratec_2026.pdf); [Regulamento 2024](https://mostratec.liberato.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/REGULAMENTO_Mostratec_2024.pdf)
- Mostratec Júnior is open to **Educação Infantil and Ensino Fundamental students "from Brazil and abroad"**. — [Mostratec Júnior page (2020 edition)](https://mostratec.liberato.com.br/en/?p=3424)

Mostratec ran a shuttle from Porto Alegre airport at **R$40/person**, and a Trensurb train option costs R$4.50. Students exhibit at stands over 3 afternoons. — [Informativo Mostratec Júnior 2024](https://mostratec.liberato.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/INFORMATIVO-MOSTRATEC-J%C3%9ANIOR-2024.pdf)

Submissions until **20 Oct 2026 18h**, and the in-person fair is in **March 2027 (15–19 Mar per a secondary source; folder shows 16–18/19 Mar)**. An advisor aged over 21 is required. The affiliated-fair rules use a 12–20 age range. — [FEBRACE 2027 folder](https://febrace.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Folder-FEBRACE-2027.pdf); [Regulamento feiras afiliadas 2027](https://febrace.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/regulamento-feiras-afiliadas-2027.pdf)

The general regulation covers research projects "in literacy and scientific initiation". — [FEBIC 2026 programme](https://www.ibicsc.com.br/assets/docs/PROGRAMA%C3%87%C3%83O%20DA%20XI%20FEBIC%20-%202026.pdf)
- **FECITEC 2026 – UFV Campus Florestal (MG, ~60 km from BH):** **24 Oct 2026 (Saturday)**. Open to teams from **Ensino Fundamental I**, Fundamental II, Médio or Técnico from any Brazilian municipality. Online registration by the advisor ran **10–30 Sep 2026**, and up to 100 projects are selected. The top 3 per level and area get certificates. Standout projects **may receive credentials to partner events: UFMG Jovem, Ciência Jovem (Recife‑PE) and the London International Youth Science Forum**. Travel funding depends on agency resources, and otherwise participants pay. — [Edital FECITEC 2026](https://fecitec.caf.ufv.br/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Edital-FECITEC-2026-.pdf)
- **UFMG Jovem (27th), BH campus Pampulha, 1–2 Oct 2026:** open to public and private MG schools including Ensino Fundamental. **Free**, and the teacher registers the project (4 May–17 Aug 2026). Up to **35 EF** and 45 EM projects are selected. — [UFMG Jovem errata 2026](https://www.ufmg.br/ufmgjovem/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Errata-UFMG-Jovem.pdf); [PROEX UFMG](https://www.ufmg.br/proex/noticia/proex-anuncia-chamada-para-a-27a-ufmg-jovem/)

### Inferences
- Realistic path: in 2027 (4º ano) the child develops a project with a teacher or advisor. Then he seeks a **Mostratec Júnior** spot via an affiliated fair or direct selection (Categoria 2, late Oct 2027, Novo Hamburgo) for a national/international trip. Families pay travel and lodging.
- Mostratec Categoria 1 is very likely 1º–3º ano, which would allow a 2026 entry, but the 2026 fair runs 26–30 Oct and the window has passed.
- FEBRACE is not relevant until about 2031 (8º ano).

### Gaps

## 6. How to enter from BH (individual vs school/team), BH schools/teachers that organize groups, and age roadmap 2026–2028

### Takeaway
Almost every Brazilian olympiad and fair (OBI, OBMEP Mirim, Canguru, OBA, OIMSF, Mostratec, UFMG Jovem, FECITEC) is **entered through the school with a teacher responsible**. The **individual/family routes** are the private international coding olympiads (Coding Olympiad/Competzy, Codeavour) and Space Camp. In BH, the documented group organizers for international trips are **public schools**: EM Cora Coralina (teacher Fábio Andrade Machado) and EM Prof. Cláudio Brandão (WMTC), and EE Maurício Murgel (MSF France). Their trips were funded by PBH or SEE/MG. No BH private school organizing such trips was identified.

### Cited Findings
- OBI: a teacher registers the school and then the students; free. — [OBI](https://olimpiada.ic.unicamp.br/)
- Canguru and OBMEP Mirim: only schools register, using the MEC/INEP code. — [Estratégia – Canguru 2026](https://vestibulares.estrategia.com/portal/noticias/canguru-matematica-brasil-2026-edital/); [Quero Bolsa – OBMEP Mirim](https://querobolsa.com.br/revista/obmep-mirim-2026)
- OIMSF: school only, one teacher‑coordinator, **no individual entries**, teams of 5 or more. — [Regulamento OIMSF 2026](https://www.matematicasemfronteiras.org/Regulamento_MSF_2026.pdf)
- OBA/OBAFOG: schools register students by 1 May. — [Educa Mais Brasil](https://www.educamaisbrasil.com.br/educacao/escolas/inscricoes-abertas-para-oba-e-obafog-2026)
- FECITEC and UFMG Jovem: the advisor or teacher registers the project. — [FECITEC edital](https://fecitec.caf.ufv.br/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Edital-FECITEC-2026-.pdf); [UFMG Jovem errata](https://www.ufmg.br/ufmgjovem/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Errata-UFMG-Jovem.pdf)
