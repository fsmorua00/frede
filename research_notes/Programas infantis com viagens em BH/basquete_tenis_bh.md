# Youth basketball and tennis in BH/RMBH with national/international travel (ages 8-11), as of 18 Oct 2026

Context for the writer: the child is 8 years old on 18 Oct 2026, so he was born between 19 Oct 2017 and 18 Oct 2018. Most Brazilian and US youth tennis events assign age by **birth year**: the age the player turns during the calendar year. If he was born in 2018, he plays "8 anos" in 2026, "9 anos" in 2027 and "10 anos" in 2028. If he was born in late 2017, he plays 9 anos in 2026 and 10 anos in 2027. Confirm his birth year before matching him to categories.

The search engine returned sparse results for local BH youth sport. Several federation sites (FMB, letzplay/FMT) were not reachable or not indexed, so some items are partial and flagged.

## 1. Basketball programs in BH/RMBH for ages 8-11, and which travel

### Takeaway
No BH program was found that clearly takes **8-year-olds** on national or international trips. The realistic BH entry points are:
- the NBA Basketball School licensee **Esporte Clube Ginástico** (Buritis; open to non-members; no tryouts);
- **Minas Tênis Clube**'s courses (NBA methodology; members' portal);
- **Olympico Club** (Serra, Centro-Sul), the strongest youth-competition club found, with state Sub-11 titles in 2023 and 2024 and interstate Sub-12 tournaments;
- **Rosso Basketball** (independent school, three units, runs mini-basketball festivals).

Interstate and international travel in Brazilian basketball typically begins at **Sub-12**, which is when he is about 11 years old.

## 2. Basketball competition ladder (FMB/CBB) and national/international events for ages 8-11, 2026-2027

### Takeaway
In MG, the state (FMB) ladder visibly starts at **Sub-11 Estadual** plus Metropolitano. National CBB interclub championships (CBIs) start at older ages: published classifiers cover Sub-15 and up.

The real **national/international mini-basketball travel options for 8-11** are private, CBB-endorsed festivals in **Foz do Iguaçu-PR**, which bring in teams from Argentina and Paraguay:
- Copa Brasil Estrelas do Futuro, Sub-8 to Sub-14 (Jul 2026);
- Minibasquete Brasil, ages 6-12 (Jul 2025).

Interstate club tournaments such as Liberty Cup (Arujá-SP) and Taça Joinville-SC start at **Sub-12**.

## 4. Tennis competition ladder for ages 8-12 (FMT, CBT, COSAT, international) and 2026-2027 calendar

### Takeaway
The ladder for an 8-year-old has six steps:

1. **FMT state kids events.** E.g., FMT KIDS Copa Futuro de Duplas, Contagem, 13-15 Nov 2026.
2. **CBT Circuito Nacional Tennis Kids** (categories 8/9/10/11, no national ranking, medals).
   - Ages 8-9 play orange ball on an 18 m court; age 10 plays green ball on a full court.

## 6. Multi-sport school games and festivals in MG/Brazil (ages 8-11)

### Takeaway
The official school games are **not open to 8-11-year-olds**. JEMG and JEBH start at **age 12** (Module I = born 2012-2014 in 2026), and the national JEBs are likewise 12+. JEBH has no court tennis (only table tennis). For ages 8-11, the travel options are sport-specific festivals: Foz mini-basketball and CBT Tennis Kids events.

### Cited Findings
- **JEMG 2026:** ages **12-17**; Module I = born **2012, 2013, 2014**; Module II = 2009-2011. Municipal registration ran 2/2-4/3/2026, and the micro-regional stage started 13/4/2026 — [Agência Minas](https://www.agenciaminas.mg.gov.br/noticia/governo-de-minas-abre-inscricoes-do-jemg-2026-para-municipios-mineiros)
- **JEBH 2026** (Belo Horizonte school games):
  - Module I = born 2012-2014; Module II = 2009-2011. Registration ran 9-20 Mar, with competitions from 10 Apr 2026.
  - Basketball (5x5 and 3x3) is included; tennis appears only as **table tennis**.
  - Source: [PBH – JEBH](https://prefeitura.pbh.gov.br/esportes-e-lazer/jogos-escolares-de-belo-horizonte); [JEBH modalidades](https://jogosdebelohorizonte.com.br/site_jebh/campeonatos.php)
- Under-12 national and international options for 8-11 are covered in sections 2 and 4: Foz mini-basketball (Sub-8 to Sub-12, with Argentina and Paraguay) and CBT Tennis Kids, Copa das Federações 9/10 and Little Mo.

### Inferences
- **School games come later.** The child cannot use school-games travel until about 2030 (Module I, age 12).
- **Until then, travel depends on the club or academy.** Ages 8-11 travel comes only through a club or academy that enters festivals and tournaments, or through family-arranged entries. Tennis Kids and Little Mo allow individual family entry.
