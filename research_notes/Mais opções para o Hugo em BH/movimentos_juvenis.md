## 1. Escoteiros do Brasil (UEB-MG), Ramo Lobinho: which out-of-town gatherings lobinhos actually attend, and when travel really starts

### Takeaway
Lobinhos (6.5–10) in BH **do** travel. Each year the three BH metropolitan districts (DMN/DMC/DMS) jointly run two lobinho gatherings outside BH:
- **AMEL**, an overnight camp in Nova Lima in September (R$300).
- **AEROLOBO**, a day event at the Air Force park in Lagoa Santa in November (R$100). It has an aviation theme.

Other MG districts run their own lobinho events, such as the Caçada de Mowgli and Elobin. These are closer to "1–2 trips a year" in the RMBH than to national trips. National and international scout gatherings start only at **11** (Jamboree Nacional) and **14** (World Jamboree). "Indaba" in MG is an adults-only meeting, not a lobinho event.

### Cited Findings
**Lobinho ages and program**
- Ramo Lobinho is "entre os 6,5 e os 10 anos". The MG site menu says "7 a 10 anos", and the transition to the Ramo Escoteiro starts before 11. The program includes "acampamentos e acantonamentos". — [UEB-MG Ramo Lobinho](https://mg.escoteiros.org.br/ramo-lobinho/); [UEB national Ramo Lobinho](https://www.escoteiros.org.br/ramo-lobinho/)
- The UEB national international calendar lists Lobinho as 6.5–10.5 years. — [UEB Calendário Internacional 2026](https://www.escoteiros.org.br/noticias/calendario-anual-escoteiro-internacional-2026/)
- The MG branch ages are Filhotes 5–6, Lobinho 7–10, Escoteiro 11–14, Sênior 15–17 and Pioneiro 18–22. — [UEB-MG](https://mg.escoteiros.org.br)

## 2. JOTA-JOTI: what it is, dates, and how BH groups (and lobinhos) take part

### Takeaway
JOTA-JOTI is WOSM's annual online and radio "jamboree", held on the third full weekend of October. The 2026 edition was **16–18 Oct 2026** (this weekend). Lobinhos take part with their own task set, from their group's base. It involves **no travel**, but it is the most tech-flavoured international scout activity open to an 8-year-old.

### Cited Findings
- JOTA-JOTI 2026 ran 16 Oct 2026 00:00 to 18 Oct 2026 23:45, as a virtual event "held every year on the third full weekend of October". It is open to "Scouts and non-Scouts of all ages". — [scout.org JOTA-JOTI 2026](https://www.scout.org/node/5693)
- UEB Boletim 2 (07/10/2026) says "Filhotes, lobinhos(as), escoteiros(as), seniores, guias e pioneiros(as) são os protagonistas". The **Ramo Lobinho had 8 tasks** published 08:00–11:00 on 17 Oct 2026. Teams are of at least 2 youths from the same branch, and the participating unit is the group (UEL). — [UEB JOTA-JOTI 2026 Boletim 2](https://www.escoteiros.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Boletim_2_jota_joti_2026.pdf)
- Non-scout youths and radio amateurs can join with a "registro provisório" (Resolução CAN 05/2024), which costs R$9.50 in 2026. — [Boletim 2](https://www.escoteiros.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Boletim_2_jota_joti_2026.pdf); [Resolução CAN 14/2025, Tabela D](https://www.escoteiros.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Resolucao-CAN-14_2025-Contribuicao-Associativa-2026.pdf)
- The radio part uses Chat, VHF, HF, UHF, EchoLink and DMR modes. It needs "radioamadores licenciados pela ANATEL e associados dos Escoteiros do Brasil". — [Boletim 2](https://www.escoteiros.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Boletim_2_jota_joti_2026.pdf)
- The WOSM ham-radio handbook (Portuguese) says LABRE regionals usually have a "Diretor de Radioescotismo" to support scout groups. — [JOTA-JOTI Ham Radio Handbook PT, 2022](https://www.scout.org/sites/default/files/2022-10/JOTA-JOTI_2022_Ham_Radio_Handbook_PT.pdf)

### Inferences
- The next edition should be the third full weekend of October 2027, which is 15–17 Oct 2027 by calendar. If Hugo joins a group in 2027 he could take part as a lobinho.

### Gaps

## 3. Jamboree Nacional and World Scout Jamboree: age ranges and hosts

### Takeaway
Both jamborees are closed to lobinhos. The Brazilian **Jamboree Nacional** is for 11–17. The next one, the 10th, is in **January 2028**, when Hugo will be 9, so he is too young. The **World Scout Jamboree 2027** is in **Poland** (Gdańsk area, 30 Jul–8 Aug 2027) for ages **14–17**. The 2031 edition is in Denmark.

### Cited Findings
- The 2024 Jamboree Nacional (centenary) was held 13–19 Jul 2024 at Parque do Peão, Barretos (SP). Youth had to be born between 13/07/2013 and 20/07/2006 ("at least 11 years old by July 13, 2024"), in Scout or Senior troops only. The fee was US$399. — [UEB Jamboree 2024 Bulletin 1](https://www.escoteiros.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Boletim_1_jamboree_do_centenario_2024_ingles.pdf)

The official bulletin above contradicts this, so trust the bulletin. — [ALEP, 23/04/2024](https://www.assembleia.pr.leg.br/comunicacao/noticias/na-celebracao-do-centenario-do-movimento-no-brasil-escoteiros-reafirmam-compromisso-de)
- The 10th Jamboree Nacional "será realizado em janeiro de 2028". The host site was still being chosen, with proposals due 10 Jun 2026. — [UEB edital 10º Jamboree](https://www.escoteiros.org.br/eventos/edital-de-indicacao-de-local-10-jamboree-nacional-escoteiro/)
- Past editions: 1998, 2003, 2006, 2009, 2012, 2015, 2018, 2020 (online) and 2024. The event is "geralmente realizado a cada três anos", with participants from 11 to under 18. — [Wikipedia PT, Jamboree Nacional Escoteiro](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamboree_Nacional_Escoteiro)
- The 26th World Scout Jamboree runs 30 Jul–8 Aug 2027. Youth participants must be "between 14 and 17", born 30 Jul 2009 to 30 Jul 2013, and 18+ can serve on the IST. — [jamboree2027.org](https://www.jamboree2027.org/stories/jamboree-dates/). ## 5. CISV (Children's International Summer Villages): chapter in BH, ages, delegations, costs

### Takeaway
**CISV has a Belo Horizonte chapter** (cisv-bhz.org.br, officebhz@cisv.org.br). It is the strongest international-travel option, but **not before age 10–11**:
- National activities start at **10**.
- The international **Village** is for children who **turn 11 during the program year**. For Hugo that means **calendar 2029**.
- Youth Meeting is for 12–13, and Step Up and Interchange are for 14–15.
- Mosaic is local and has no age requirement.

Fees are set by each chapter and are not published.

### Cited Findings
- CISV Brasil chapters: Araraquara, **Belo Horizonte** (cisv-bhz.org.br), Brasília, Campinas, Londrina, Rio de Janeiro, Salvador, São José dos Campos, São Paulo and Vitória. — [CISV Brasil](https://www.cisv.org.br)
- FAQ: "Nossas atividades e programas nacionais são para crianças e jovens a partir de 10 anos", and international programs start at 11. Membership has an annual fee. "Para líderes e staffs não é cobrada taxa de participação". BH contact: officebhz@cisv.org.br. — [CISV BH FAQ](https://cisv-bhz.org.br/faq)
- **Village:** each delegation is mixed, with 4 children "que podem completar 11 anos em qualquer momento do ano do programa" plus a leader aged 21+. The International Village lasts 28 days and the National 15 days. — [CISV BH Village](https://cisv-bhz.org.br/pagina/village)
- **Conflict on length:** CISV Brasil says the international Village lasts 23 days. — [CISV Brasil Village](https://cisv.org.br/pagina/village)
- The international site says Villages "bring 11 years olds together", with delegations from 10–12 countries. — [cisv.org Village](https://cisv.org/programmes/village/)
- **Youth Meeting:** "jovens de 12 à 13 anos", national or international, up to 15 days. — [CISV BH Youth Meeting](https://cisv-bhz.org.br/pagina/youth-meeting)
- **Step Up:** delegates "podem completar 14 ou 15 anos em qualquer momento do ano do programa". It lasts 23 days international or 15 days national. — [CISV BH Step Up](https://cisv-bhz.org.br/pagina/step-up)
- **Interchange:** "devem ter de 14 à 15 anos". It is a two-phase family exchange, with each phase lasting 2–4 weeks. — [CISV BH Interchange](https://cisv-bhz.org.br/pagina/interchange)
- **Mosaic:** "não há pré-requisitos de idade". These are local projects run by chapter volunteers. — [CISV BH Mosaic](https://cisv-bhz.org.br/pagina/mosaic)
- Joining starts by contacting the chapter. Fees and dues are given by the chapter. Children under 16 travel with an adult aged 18+ (national) or 21+ (international). Member families can join local activities and events. — [CISV BH, Sua família e o CISV](https://cisv-bhz.org.br/pagina/sua-famlia-e-o-cisv)
- US chapters charge between US$1,725 and US$4,500 for a 2026 Village, but this is not a guide for Brazil. — [CISV Columbus](https://columbus.cisvusa.org/program/village/); [CISV New York](https://newyork.cisvusa.org/program/village/)

### Inferences
- Hugo turns 11 on 9 Nov 2029. Under the "any time in the program year" rule he is a Village candidate for **programs held in 2029**, so the earliest is a mid-2029 Village abroad at age 10. National CISV activities open from age 10 (Nov 2028). Joining the BH chapter as a family in 2027–2028 (local activities, Mosaic) would position him for delegation selection.
- From 11 to 15, CISV offers a ladder: Village (11) → Youth Meeting (12–13) → Step Up and Interchange (14–15).

### Gaps
- BH chapter fees (annual dues, Village participation fee, airfare policy) and the delegate selection process are not published. Ask officebhz@cisv.org.br.
- It is not confirmed whether CISV BH runs regular local activities for under-10s.
