# Other competitive activities with real travel for Hugo (8–9, BH Centro-Sul). Excludes robotics, chess, tennis, basketball, coding and math

Scope: activities in BH and its metro area where children aged 8–9 actually travel once or twice a year to national or international events. Checked on 18/10/2026.

**Age note.** Hugo was born on 09/11/2018 (Brazilian date format), so he is 7 today and turns 8 on 9 Nov 2026. Most federations below count age as "age reached during the calendar year". That makes him **8 for all of 2026 and 9 for all of 2027** in kart, jiu-jitsu, equestrian, judô and Pokémon. U.S. Kids Golf is different: it uses a tournament age date, so he would play its 31 Oct 2026 event as a 7-year-old.

## Q1. What FITS NOW: age 8–9 with real national or international travel?

### Takeaway
Seven activities are open to Hugo now and have verified national or international events. Ranked by fit for a tech-loving kid:
1. Speedcubing (WCA): cheap, and the Brazilian championship is in Goiânia in November 2026.
2. Kart: his age band, Mirim, is exactly 8–9 in 2026, but it is very expensive.
3. Pokémon TCG/VGC: the Latin America International Championships are in São Paulo in November 2026. Whether the Junior division runs in Brazil in 2027 is disputed between sources.
4. Junior golf: a U.S. Kids Golf qualifier is held in Brasília and leads toward Pinehurst.
5. Jiu-jitsu: the CBJJ Brasileiro Kids is in Barueri (SP). Pan Kids in the USA needs a gray belt or higher.
6. Show jumping: the CBH Brasileiro de Iniciantes accepts riders from the year they turn 7.
7. Sailing: Optimist nationals take ages 7–15, but I found no verified Optimist school in BH.

Football and futsal have sub-9 travel tournaments, but I could not confirm that a BH escolinha travels with sub-9 teams.
