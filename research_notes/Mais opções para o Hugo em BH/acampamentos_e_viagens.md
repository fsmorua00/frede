# Holiday camps and group trips (Brazil and abroad) for Hugo, 8–9, from BH: tech/STEM priority

Context used for all verdicts: Hugo was born 09/11/2018 (9 Nov 2018), so he is **8 in Jan 2027 and Jul 2027, 9 in Jan and Jul 2028, and 10 in Jan and Jul 2029**. (This is plain date arithmetic. Today is 18/10/2026.) "Fits now" means the Jan/Jul 2027 holidays.

## 1. Holiday camps in Brazil popular with BH families (age, tech themes, Jan/Jul 2027 dates, prices, transport from BH)

### Takeaway
No overnight holiday camp in Brazil that we found is tech or robotics themed. The classic camps (Peraltas, NR, Carroção, Paiol Grande) take an 8-year-old alone. **Peraltas is the only one with real science content**: its own astronomy and science centre, the C.E.U., with a planetarium session included. **None of them runs transport from BH.** Every group bus leaves from São Paulo, so a BH parent has to deliver Hugo to SP or to the camp gate.

### Cited Findings
**Peraltas (Brotas-SP): best science fit**
- Seasons run in the January and July holidays, with 5–7-day packages and longer stays possible. The camp offers "transporte saindo de SP", food, 24h monitors, an infirmary with a nurse, a hospital under 2 km away, health insurance, ecotourism and "atividades no Centro de Estudos do Universo" (C.E.U.). The C.E.U. sessions are multimedia lessons about the universe. — [Peraltas official](https://peraltas.com.br/acampamento-de-ferias/)
- The official page still lists only the July 2026 dates: 12–19 Jul (8 days) and 19–24 Jul (6 days), plus a 10–12 Jul weekend without transport and a 6–9 Jul day colony with no overnight stay. **Jan 2027 dates were not yet published** as of 18/10/2026. — [Peraltas official](https://peraltas.com.br/acampamento-de-ferias/)
- January 2026 season (most recent prices): 9–18 Jan. The 6-day package (9–14/01) was R$ 5,446 and the 5-day package (14–18/01) was R$ 4,654. Both included a planetarium star-observation session at the CEU ("one of Latin America's largest astronomy and science centres", on the camp grounds), São Paulo–Brotas transport, travel insurance, an R$ 120 canteen credit and 4 meals/day. Siblings and referrals get discounts. — [ABC Repórter, 15/12/2025](https://abcreporter.com.br/2025/12/15/acampamento-peraltas-lanca-temporada-unindo-diversao-experiencias-e-aprendizado/)
- In Nov 2025 these were promotional prices for November sign-ups. Accepted ages are 4–16. — [São Paulo para Crianças](https://saopauloparacriancas.com.br/acampamento-peraltas-ferias-janeiro/) (via search snippet)
- Phone (14) 3653-9998 / (11) 3035-1900. — [ABC Repórter](https://abcreporter.com.br/2025/12/15/acampamento-peraltas-lanca-temporada-unindo-diversao-experiencias-e-aprendizado/); [Peraltas](https://peraltas.com.br/acampamento-de-ferias/)

**NR (Nosso Recanto), Sapucaí-Mirim-MG and Santo Antônio do Pinhal-SP**
- Two resorts of its own. NR runs holiday camps, graduation trips, educational trips, exchanges and sports camps. — [NR official](https://www.nr.com.br/)
- Price range R$ 7,900–8,100, including 24h doctors, nursing, 5 meals/day, all activities and monitors in the rooms. — [CNN Brasil, 09/01/2026](https://www.cnnbrasil.com.br/viagemegastronomia/viagem/colonia-de-ferias-10-acampamentos-perto-de-sp-para-tirar-filhos-da-rotina/)
- Ages 5–10 go to the Sapucaí-Mirim (MG) base, and older groups (7–13, 11–16) to Santo Antônio do Pinhal. Round-trip transport from São Paulo is included. NR has also run a themed "NR Basketball" season. — older listings via search snippets ([Guia da Semana](https://www.guiadasemana.com.br/filhos/noticia/ferias-para-os-pais); [São Paulo para Crianças](https://saopauloparacriancas.com.br/?p=222148)). Not verified on the official site, which shows no 2027 dates or prices.

**Robotics-competition trips (the "Rio trip" pattern)**
- SESI has been Brazil's official FIRST LEGO League operator since 2013. FLL Challenge is for ages 9–16 in Brazil. Regional tournaments in several states feed a national round, which is part of the Festival SESI in Brasília (Feb/Mar). National champions go to international events. — [Agência CNI de Notícias](https://noticias.portaldaindustria.com.br/noticias/robotica/comecam-os-torneios-regionais-de-robotica-da-fllc-confira-o-calendario/); [Festival SESI 2024](https://noticias.portaldaindustria.com.br/noticias/educacao/festival-sesi-de-educacao-270-equipes-reunidas-em-brasilia-para-o-maior-torneio-de-robotica-do-pais/)
- MG holds its own regional FLL tournament, which feeds national slots. — [Agência CNI: MG, PA e PE encerram regionais](https://noticias.portaldaindustria.com.br/noticias/robotica/mg-pa-e-pe-encerram-regionais-de-robotica-com-14-vagas-para-o-nacional/)
- The FLL "Explore" category (younger kids) exists on the Brazilian circuit: a Porto Seguro school team presented in Explore at the 2025 World Festival in Houston. — [Colégio Porto Seguro FLL](https://fll.portoseguro.org.br/?p=1033)
- OBR (Olimpíada Brasileira de Robótica): in one school's 2025 circular, Nível 1 of the theory track covers 2º–3º ano. The practical track has state and national phases; the 2025 national was 13–19 Oct in Vitória-ES. — [Marista Natal circular](https://marista.edu.br/natal/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/019-DIR-OLIMPIADA-DE-ROBOTICA-OBR-MODALIDADE-TEORICA.pdf); [Governo ES](https://www.es.gov.br/Noticia/alunos-da-rede-estadual-vencem-etapa-da-olimpiada-brasileira-de-robotica-obr)

**Legal note for domestic travel**
- A child under 16 can travel in Brazil with an adult other than a parent, without a court order, if a notarised private authorisation is provided. — [TJ/MG via Sedep](https://www.sedep.com.br/?p=103484)

### Inferences
- For a screen- and tech-loving 8-year-old, **Peraltas is the clear first choice in Brazil**: the C.E.U. astronomy centre and planetarium are part of the package, it takes ages from 4, it runs Jan and Jul seasons of 5–7 days, and it costs about R$ 4.6–5.4k (Jan 2026 prices). The others are excellent classic camps, but some deliberately exclude electronics (Paiol, Carroção).
- BH logistics: every operator bus leaves from São Paulo. Options are to fly BH→SP and use the camp bus, or to drive to the camp (Sapucaí-Mirim and São Bento do Sapucaí, both in the Serra da Mantiqueira, are the closest to BH). A practical idea is to recruit 2–4 DHEL/school families to go together.
- The trip format Hugo loved (robotics class travelling to an event) maps onto the FLL/OBR circuit. Asking DHEL whether they field FLL Explore now and FLL Challenge from age 9 may give the 1–2 group trips a year with the most tech content.

### Verdict (Brazil)
- **FITS NOW (8):** Peraltas (science/astronomy); NR Sapucaí-Mirim (5–10 group); Sítio do Carroção (Kids 5–9); Paiol Grande (Jan 2027 dates out); robotics-competition trips with DHEL (FLL Explore / OBR Nível 1, depending on what DHEL enters).
- **FITS LATER:** FLL Challenge (from 9, i.e. the 2027/28 season, if the age is counted at his birthday in Nov 2027). Timbalaia's 10–17 track with the "zero-gravity simulator" (from 10).
- **DOESN'T FIT:** MPC Nova Lima (family camp, parents present); BH and Rio tech "colônias" (day-only, not a trip); camps near BH in Brumadinho, Itabirito or Serra do Cipó (no kids-alone camp found).

### Gaps
- Jan 2027 and Jul 2027 dates and prices for Peraltas, NR and Carroção: not yet published as text (Carroção shows them only as images). Contact the camps.
- "Acampamento Tia Nina" and "Acampamento Monteiro Lobato": no source found. The closest match is Acampamento dos Pumas, which visits Monteiro Lobato's house.
- No overnight kids-alone holiday camp in the BH metro area found with public info. It may exist via schools, churches or scouting groups, but nothing appeared in search.
- NR ages per unit, the NR Basketball season and NR transport come only from older third-party listings.
- FLL Challenge age rule (age on which date?) and whether DHEL competes in FLL/OBR: not confirmed.

## 2. Tech camps abroad for ages 7–10 (min age, alone vs parent, price, dates, Brazilian group organisers)

### Takeaway
At 8, no reputable overnight tech camp abroad that we found takes Hugo alone with a Brazilian group. **Space Camp (Huntsville) is the standout and fits from July 2028 (age 9)**: Space Camp, Space Camp Robotics and Aviation Challenge Mach I all take ages 9–11 for $1,699, with airport pickup and unaccompanied-minor (UM) gate pickup. **iD Tech is overnight only from age 10**; for 7–9 it is day-camp only. In Switzerland, the STEM tracks start at 10.

### Cited Findings
**Space Camp, U.S. Space & Rocket Center (Huntsville, AL)**
- Programs and ages:
  - Space Camp: ages 9–11, $1,699.
  - **Space Camp Robotics: 9–11, $1,699** (next session 30/05–04/06/2027).
  - Aviation Challenge Mach I: 9–11, $1,699.
  - Space Academy: 12–14.
  - **Family Space Camp: 7–18+, $699–799 per person** (3-day format).
  - Weeklong/Pathfinder group programs: 9–18+, 16+ participants.
  — [rocketcenter.com/spacecamp](https://rocketcenter.com/spacecamp)
- Age rule: "Nine year olds must have completed or currently be enrolled in the 4th grade." 2027 Space Camp weeks are Mar 14–19, then weekly from May 30 to Sep 3, including **4–9, 11–16, 18–23 and 25–30 July 2027**. **No January sessions are listed.** — [Space Camp program page](https://rocketcenter.com/spacecamp/space-camp/)
- Weeklong overnight campers can be picked up at Huntsville airport (HSV, about 15 min away) for a fee: latest pages say $120 round trip, older ones $50–100. Unaccompanied minors are met at the arrival gate by staff. Contact: transportation@spacecamp.com. — [Space Camp FAQ: airport pickup](https://www.spacecamp.com/help/FAQ/airportpickup) (via search snippet)
- Family Space Camp needs at least one adult. Adult and family 3-day programmes no longer include shuttle service. — [Space Camp FAQ](https://www.spacecamp.com/help/FAQ/airportpickup) (via search snippet); [She Buys Travel guide](https://shebuystravel.com/the-complete-guide-to-space-camp-alabama/) (via search snippet)
- Brazilian organiser: Experimento runs a "NASA Space Camp" group (Florida) on **8–17 Jan 2027, for ages 14–18** with intermediate/advanced English. — [Experimento](https://www.experimento.com.br/estudo/programas-de-ferias/grupos-experimento/nasa-space-camp/) (via search snippet; site returned 403)
- No Brazilian agency was found running Space Camp groups for ages 9–11. — searches in PT/EN ([example result set](https://shebuystravel.com/the-complete-guide-to-space-camp-alabama/))

**iD Tech (US university campuses)**
- Weeklong camps for ages 7–17 with "day and overnight options". Camps start at $949/week. **Summer '27 registration opens to the public on 8 Nov 2026.** — [iD Tech overnight camps](https://www.idtech.com/overnight-summer-camps)

### Inferences
- **Best international target: Space Camp Robotics or Space Camp in July 2028** (Hugo 9, and in 4º ano if his schooling follows the standard path, which should satisfy the 4th-grade rule; confirm with camps@spacecamp.com). July 2027 weeks match Brazilian winter holidays, and January does not.
- A bridge for July 2027 would be **Family Space Camp (7+) with a parent**, $699–799 each for 3 days. It is not a "without parents" experience, but it is a strong tech hook.
- **iD Tech** works for him alone only from age 10. Under the Dec-31 rule that might be summer 2028, since he turns 10 in Nov 2028, but overnight may require the age by the start date, which would mean summer 2029. Ask iD Tech before relying on it. Until then, iD Tech would require a parent staying nearby (day camp, $949+/week).
- Swiss options are expensive (CHF 5–6.5k for 2 weeks plus flights) and the STEM parts start at 10. At 8 he would be a solo UM flyer to Geneva with no Portuguese-speaking group, which is a poor fit now.

### Verdict (abroad, tech)
- **FITS NOW (8), with a parent only:** Family Space Camp (7+); iD Tech day camp (7–9 Minecraft/Roblox/Coding; parent must stay in the city).
- **FITS LATER:** Space Camp, Space Camp Robotics, Aviation Challenge Mach I (from 9, July 2028); iD Tech overnight (from 10, summer 2028 or 2029); Village Camps STEM & Robotics (from 10, July 2029); Les Elfes Robotics workshop (from 10); Experimento NASA Space Camp group (from 14).
- **DOESN'T FIT:** Space Camp in January (no Jan sessions); Helvetic Institute (day-only); Emagination (cannot verify it still exists); Les Elfes and St. George's at 8 (accepted, but solo intercontinental travel, no PT group, STEM only from 10, CHF 5k+).

## 3. Junior international programs (intercâmbio infantil) from Brazil/BH accepting 8–10-year-olds

### Takeaway
Only **EF's "Acampamento Infantil" (7–9 / 7–13)** clearly accepts an 8-year-old in an escorted group from Brazil. It is a language camp, not STEM. Experimento groups start at 9, and CI, Disney-teen and most others at 12.

### Cited Findings
- EF Acampamento Infantil: "adequado para idade 7–9" (the programme overall covers 7–13). It lasts 1–4 weeks, starting in June, July or August. It includes "voos e traslados com um Líder EF", with the leader staying with the group from departure to return and staff on site 24/7. The week has 20 language lessons and 10 activities/excursions. **No STEM content**; prices on request; departure cities not stated. — [EF Kids Camps](https://www.ef.com.br/ils-jr/courses/kids-camps/); [EF ILS Jr](https://www.ef.com.br/ils-jr/)
- Experimento: holiday programmes for **ages 9–18**, with a full-time leader on group trips at a 1:15 ratio. — [Experimento Programas de Férias](https://www.experimento.com.br/estudo/programas-de-ferias/) (via search snippet; site returned 403)
- CI Intercâmbio Teen groups are for **12–17**, with a Brazilian CI leader from departure to return. Groups cost from R$ 17,000 (individual from R$ 8,000), with Jan 2027 and Jul 2027 departures. There are no robotics programmes. — [CI Intercâmbio Teen](https://www.ci.com.br/intercambio-teen/)
- Águia Intercâmbios groups (with tour leader) are aimed at adolescents from 12. — [Águia](https://aguiaoperadora.com.br/intercambio-para-adolescentes-e-criancas/) (via search snippet)
- Disney trips without parents:
  - Mister Disney: from 12 years complete; under-12s only with a legal guardian or an adult over 21. — [Mister Disney](https://misterdisney.com.br/pacote-de-viagem/) (via search snippet)
  - Aerotur Teen: recommends 12+. — [Aerotur](https://aerotur.com.br/pacotes/disney-magica-2026/) (via search snippet)
  - Most agencies take 11–17. — [Dicas da Flórida](https://dicasdaflorida.com/orlando/melhores-agencias-de-viagem-para-criancas-e-adolescentes-em-orlando/) (via search snippet)

### Inferences
- Escorted group trips abroad from Brazil effectively start at 9 (Experimento) to 12 (CI, Disney teen). EF Kids Camp is the only escorted option at 8, and it does not match the tech priority.
- The realistic international path is a parent-escorted trip to Space Camp at 9, then iD Tech or Village Camps at 10+, then CI or Experimento STEM-flavoured teen groups at 12–14.

### Verdict (junior exchange)
- **FITS NOW (8):** EF Acampamento Infantil (escorted group, language camp, not STEM).
- **FITS LATER:** Experimento groups (from 9; NASA Space Camp group from 14); CI Intercâmbio Teen (from 12); Disney teen groups (from 11–12).
- **DOESN'T FIT:** none ruled out beyond the age limits above.
