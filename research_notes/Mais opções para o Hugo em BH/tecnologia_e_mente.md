## 3. Math olympiads with international finals for ages 7–10: organisers in Brazil, BH participants

### Takeaway
The clearest travelling path for a 2º/3º ano child is the **SIMCC pipeline (SASMO in April or AMO in October, taken online from home) leading to SIMOC in Singapore (July)**. Its Brazilian representative is **Mathletes – Olimpíadas Internacionais**. In 2026, 36 Brazilian students went to SIMOC, **7 of them in grades 1–3**. No BH centre was found that prepares young kids for international finals. BH's public "Trilhas Olímpicas" programme funds such trips only for municipal-school students.

### Cited Findings
#### VERIFIED
- **Mathletes – Olimpíadas Internacionais** (olimpiadasinternacionais.org) represents SIMCC in Brazil. Contact: WhatsApp +55 11 93908-1034, contato@olimpiadasinternacionais.org, Instagram @olimpiadasinternacionais. — [olimpiadasinternacionais.org](https://olimpiadasinternacionais.org/); [O Povo](https://www.opovo.com.br/noticias/ceara/2026/07/27/amp/estudante-do-ceara-conquista-duas-medalhas-em-singapura.html) (Mathletes' pedagogical director Marina Bronzeri coordinated the delegation as Brazil's official representative)
- **SASMO (qualifier):**
  - Grades: 1º ano EF to 3º EM.
  - Online format: taken at home on Zoom with the camera on and a second monitoring device; there is a mandatory test call on 1 April.
  - In-school format: needs at least 10 students.
  - Fee and dates: R$139.90; registration until 30 March, exam 3–5 April.
  - Awards: medals go to the top 8% (gold), 12% (silver) and 20% (bronze).
  - Source: [Mathletes SASMO](https://olimpiadasinternacionais.org/sasmo/)
- **AMO – American Math Olympiad (qualifier):**
  - Grades: 2º ano EF upward.
  - Format: online or printed at school; 25 questions in 90 minutes.
  - Fee and dates: R$149.90; 2026 exam in mid/late October (the page gives conflicting dates: 17–19 or 23–25 October).
  - Invitations: all award winners are invited to the SMC Global Finals (online) and to SIMOC (Singapore); gold and silver medallists are also invited to STEAM Ahead (December 2026).
  - Source: [Mathletes AMO](https://olimpiadasinternacionais.org/amo/)
- **SIMOC 2026 (Singapore, in person):**
  - Dates: 17–22/07/2026, for SIMCC medallists.

- **Children Hugo's age went:** the Brazilian SIMOC 2026 delegation had 36 students from 21 schools: 1 in Grade 1, 2 in Grade 2 and 4 in Grade 3. Several won Maths Warriors gold or bronze. No cities are given. — [Mathletes SIMOC 2026](https://olimpiadasinternacionais.org/simoc-2026/)

- For Hugo (2º ano in 2026, 3º ano in 2027): SASMO in April 2027, taken online from BH, could lead to SIMOC in Singapore in July 2027, roughly US$1,700 for him plus US$1,350–1,550 for a parent, before flights. ## 6. Science / astronomy / rockets (clubs in BH; MOBFOG/OBAFOG, Mostratec Júnior, Febrace, astronomy meetings)

### Takeaway
For ages 7–10, the national science event that **actually admits young children and that families travel to** is **Mostratec Júnior** (Novo Hamburgo, RS, late October), which has Educação Infantil and Ensino Fundamental categories. Getting there from BH requires a school project, typically credentialed by an affiliated fair; the MG fair FEMIC credentials projects, but its rules for young grades were not found. OBA and OBAFOG take children from 1º ano, but at their level everything happens at school, with no travel. In BH, the Espaço do Conhecimento UFMG, a few blocks from Centro-Sul, offers kids' astronomy activities but does not travel.

### Cited Findings
#### VERIFIED
- **Mostratec-Liberato 2026 (41st edition):** 26–30/10/2026 at FENAC, Novo Hamburgo (RS). The 2026 regulation lists **Educação Infantil** and Ensino Fundamental categories 1–4 under Mostratec Júnior. — [Regulamento Mostratec 2026](https://mostratec.liberato.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/REGULAMENTO_Mostratec_2026.pdf). This URL returned 404 when re-fetched; the content comes from the search index. A Faccat selection document from an earlier year also schedules a "Mostra de Educação Infantil" on the fair's last day. — [Faccat](https://www2.faccat.br/portal/sites/default/files/criterios_selecao_mostratec_junior.pdf)
- **Out-of-state families do travel to it:** the 2024 Mostratec Júnior guide for Ensino Fundamental covers flights into Porto Alegre, an airport shuttle (R$40), train and app-ride options, stand set-up, two days of judging and an awards ceremony. — [Informativo Mostratec Júnior 2024](https://mostratec.liberato.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/INFORMATIVO-MOSTRATEC-JÚNIOR-2024.pdf)
