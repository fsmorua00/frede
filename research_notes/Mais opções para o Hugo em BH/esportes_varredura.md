# Tennis and basketball programs in BH/RMBH (incl. Nova Lima) whose 8–10-year-olds travel to out-of-town events: reverse sweep from entry lists (as of 18 Oct 2026)

**Hugo's age and categories**

Hugo was born 09/11/2018, read as **9 Nov 2018** in Brazilian DD/MM format.
- On 18 Oct 2026 he is **7**, and he turns 8 on 9 Nov 2026. The earlier note's "8 years old on 18 Oct 2026" is off by a few weeks.
- Tennis (CBT/FMT) assigns age by birth year, so a 2018-born player is in **"8 anos" in 2026**, **"9 anos" in 2027** and "10 anos" in 2028. Ages 8–9 play orange ball; age 10 plays green ball.

**Method**

I scraped every 7/8-, 8-, 9- and 10-year-old entry list for 32 tournaments (2025–2026) on Tênis Integrado, the CBT/FMT entry system:
- the FMT KIDS stages;
- the CBT Tennis Kids stages (Betim, Rio);
- Banana Bowl kids 2026;
- Brasileirão 2025/2026;
- Copa São Paulo 2026;
- the Circuito Sudeste stages;
- Bahia 2025/2026;
- Cuiabá, Campinas, and the Rio state kids circuit.

From each list I kept players whose city is Belo Horizonte, Nova Lima, Contagem or Lagoa Santa, together with the **club/entity label** printed under their name.

Counts below were computed by me from those public lists, and I cite the list URLs. Children's names are deliberately not reproduced here.

"Distinct kids per club" means each child is assigned the label they carry most often across all scraped events. Per-event links point to the category lists, where the label printed on that entry can be checked.

**Caveat:** the club label is optional and self-declared. It is the FMT-affiliated entity, not necessarily the academy where the child trains. Most RMBH kids, 142 of the distinct kids found at FMT/CBT events, have **no label**.

## 1. Tennis: which BH/Nova Lima programs have 8–10-year-olds at out-of-town events (reverse lookup from entry lists)

### Takeaway
BH is far from limited to Fly Sports. In 2025–2026 there was a large, active circuit of RMBH kids aged 7–10 travelling to out-of-town events. Five labelled programs/clubs had 8–10-year-olds at events outside the BH metro area:
- **Minas Tênis Clube**, by far the largest;
- **Fly Sports** (label "Fly Sports MoriahLab", plus one "Arena Fly Sports");
- **Barroca Tênis Clube / One Tennis**;
- **Casalechi Tennis**;
- **Pampulha Iate Clube**.

The trips were to Divinópolis, Pará de Minas, Gov. Valadares, Araguari, Uberlândia, São Paulo, São José do Rio Preto, Rio, Salvador, Campinas and Cuiabá. Many more unlabelled BH kids did the same, entered by their families or academies.

### Inferences
- **Reading the labels.**
  - "Fly Sports MoriahLab" is very likely the FMT-affiliated entity of Fly Sports. The name matches, and Fly's registered seat is in Vila da Serra, Nova Lima ([Econodata](https://www.econodata.com.br/consulta-empresa/13050103000108-fly-sports-ltda)). I could not find what "MoriahLab" refers to (inference).
  - "Casalechi Tennis" kids are likely tied to the Casalechi/One Tennis group, which also runs the Barroca kids school (see Q2).
- **The travelling circuit is larger than any program list.**
  - About 140 unlabelled RMBH kids played FMT KIDS events, and dozens went to Divinópolis, Pará de Minas and Uberlândia.
  - Their academies cannot be identified from the lists. They are probably spread across the academies in Q2 plus private coaches.
- **Practical trip ladder for a BH 8–9-year-old, as observed (not a published program):**
  - **In MG, 1–3 trips/year:** FMT KIDS interior stages (Divinópolis in June, Pará de Minas in April, Gov. Valadares in May, Araguari in August) and the Brasileirão Kids in Uberlândia in July.
  - **Out of state:** Banana Bowl kids (March), Copa SP (January), CBT Tennis Kids Rio (October), Bahia (November), SP/RJ state kids circuits.
- **Fit to the 1–2 trips/year goal.**
  - The Divinópolis Grand Slam Kids (June) plus the Brasileirão Kids (Uberlândia, July) is the most common pattern among BH kids aged 8–10. Both are in MG.
  - Banana Bowl (SP, March) is the most common out-of-state choice.

### Gaps
- **Coach travel.** No source shows whether coaches from any program travel with the 8–10-year-olds, or how costs are shared. Minas's article calls the group a "delegação" but names no accompanying coach.
- **Unlabelled kids' academies** cannot be determined from Tênis Integrado. Player profiles show tournament history but not academy.
- **"MoriahLab"** could not be resolved.
- **Girls' entries** were included in the scrape, but I tallied club labels mainly from boys' categories. Few RMBH girls appeared in national 8–10 draws.
