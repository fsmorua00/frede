## 1. Which BH/RMBH schools, academies or teams appear in competition participant and result lists (2024-2026)?

### Takeaway
Two colégios outside DHEL put children of Hugo's age into an out-of-town national final in 2026, both through the MG FIRST LEGO League Explore festival:
- **Colégio Sagrado Coração de Jesus** (Savassi), in the 1º-2º ano category;
- **Colégio Logosófico, Funcionários unit**, in the 3º-5º ano category.

Both qualified for the national stage in Rio on 17/10/2026. In OBR, **Colégio Bernoulli** dominates BH's Nível 1 field (14 of 24 Rescue N1 teams in 2026) and has one team going to the national in João Pessoa.

Every other list (FLL Challenge, TBR, FIRA, WRO, MNR) shows **no BH independent academy besides DHEL**. The FLL Challenge lists are dominated by SESI and public schools.
